# Sources and modelling decisions

Reviewed 6 October 2026 for a synthetic reporting cutoff of 31 December 2025. Current webpages do not prove that identical underwriting criteria applied in 2025. The model's risk limits and interest cap are owner-approved assumptions.

## Bank underwriting examples

- Unibank, dated 1 May 2025: “Kredit almaq üçün müraciət edilə bilər. Lakin kreditin verilib-verilməməsi bank tərəfindən müəyyənləşdiriləcək.” Poor-history applicants may apply; bank discretion remains. No published fixed waiting period here. https://unibank.az/az/customerQuestions/kridit-tarixcesi
- ABB: age, income and credit history enter online lending assessments. No stated post-arrears waiting period on this page. https://abb-bank.az/onlayn-kredit
- Atom Bank residential mortgage criteria: missed mortgage/secured-loan payments within the last 12 months lead to decline under the cited criteria. Product and adverse-credit category matter. This is a lookback, not a universal wait after payoff. https://www.atombank.co.uk/intermediaries/residential/lending-criteria/
- Aldermore residential mortgage criteria illustrate differing clean-history windows (including six and eighteen months) by product tier. https://www.aldermore.co.uk/intermediaries/mortgages/latest-updates/backing-more-of-your-clients-to-go-for-it/
- ASB personal lending: monthly repayment limited to 45% of income on the cited page; other requirements apply. https://asb.az/az/ferdi/kreditler/ISTEHLAK-KREDITI-Personal
- Bank of Ireland first-time-buyer mortgages: gross annual income multiples and property-value limits, not a direct net monthly income percentage comparison. https://personalbanking.bankofireland.com/borrow/mortgages/first-time-buyer/
- Emirates NBD discusses total monthly debt burden in the UAE context. https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/knowledge-hub/plan-payments-the-smart-way

These sources motivate assessing past repayment behaviour, income and existing debt. They do not establish our 35/25/15/10% tiers or a mandatory Azerbaijani 12-month exclusion.

## Azerbaijan taxes

- State Tax Service profit-tax guide: 20% of taxable profit and annual reporting/advance-payment framework. https://taxes.gov.az/az/page/vergi-beledcisi
- 2025 profit-tax booklet, including building depreciation up to 7%: https://www.taxes.gov.az/uploads/2025/buklet/26.pdf
- State Tax Service answer dated 17 November 2025, rental income and 14% withholding for resident individual landlords: https://taxes.gov.az/az/page/suallar-ve-cavablar?page=7
- Resident-company rental income excluded from this withholding under Article 124.3: https://taxes.gov.az/az/page/suallar-ve-cavablar?page=11
- Enterprise property-tax overview (older official explanatory guide): https://taxes.gov.az/uploads/2017/PV_new/emlak.pdf ; official Article 198.2 explanation: https://www.taxes.gov.az/az/page/suallar-ve-cavablar?page=50
- Financial-services VAT exemption is activity-specific, not a blanket exemption for everything a bank buys or sells: https://taxes.gov.az/az/page/suallar-ve-cavablar?page=262

The supplied tax bridge is an educational estimate. Legal lease classification, allowable deductions, actual advance payments and full asset scope need independent evidence before real filing. No fictional tax return is represented as compliant.

## Accounting and interest-cap distinction

- IFRS 16 overview: right-of-use asset and lease liability for leases in scope. https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-16-leases/
- IFRS 16 standard, initial present value and subsequent measurement: https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/pdf-standards/english/2022/issued/part-a/ifrs-16-leases.pdf?bypass=on
- Azerbaijan Civil Code amendment discusses a 180-day late-interest limit for applicable consumer credit, rather than certifying this project's 90-day freeze of all interest: https://president.az/az/articles/view/59273
- CBA rules distinguish delinquency/asset classification: https://www.cbar.az/law-30/decision-no-1851100014?language=az

The project's 90-day all-interest freeze must be labelled an agreed simulation policy. It must not be presented as verified legal compliance, IFRS non-accrual accounting, or an automatic debt write-off.
